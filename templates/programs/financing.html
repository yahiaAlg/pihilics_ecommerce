Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible for financing?
Residents of Algeria, age 18+, with a valid national ID (CNIB) and regular, documented income. A soft eligibility check is performed by our financing partner — it won't affect your standing with your bank.
Is there a down payment?
A down payment is optional. You can finance 100% of the purchase price or make a partial down payment to reduce your monthly installments.
Can I pay off early?
Yes. You can pay off the remaining balance at any time with no early repayment fees or penalties.
